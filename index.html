How to read this

Unit
Your standard bet size. Here 1 unit = $10. Stronger edges get more units (max 3.0).
Spread
Betting on a team to win by more than (-) or lose by less than (+) the number. Alabama -7.5 must win by 8+.
Total (Over/Under)
Betting on the combined points of both teams being over or under the number.
Moneyline (ML)
Betting on a team to win outright. -150 means risk $150 to win $100; +130 means risk $100 to win $130.
Win chance
The model's calibrated probability the bet wins. '57%' means bets like this have historically won about 57% of the time.
Edge / EV
Expected profit per dollar if the win chance is right. +5% EV on a $10 bet = +$0.50 on average, not every time.
Break-even
The win rate you need to not lose money at that price. At -110 it's 52.4%.
Tiers
Strong = biggest edge with backtest support; Solid = good edge; Lean = small edge (smaller stake).
Edge gone
The line moved after the bet was recommended and the model no longer sees value. If you haven't placed it yet, skip it.
Parlay
Several bets combined; all must win. Pays more but loses more often. Only shown when the math still favors you.
Push
Exact tie with the line (e.g. win by exactly 7 on -7). Your stake is refunded.
Kalman / HMM
Quant-trading math: a Kalman filter tracks each team's hidden true strength; Hidden Markov Models estimate whether a team's offense and defense are in a breakout, normal or slump regime.
Change point
A detector that notices when a team suddenly starts playing differently (new QB, scheme change...), so old games stop counting as much.
Luck score
Actual margin minus 'process' margin. The process margin uses expected points from how each drive was played (field position, how deep it got), not how it ended. Big positive luck = the scoreboard flattered the team.
Outcome range
Instead of one predicted score, the model gives the full spread of likely outcomes. It accounts for key numbers like 3 and 7, and some games are more unpredictable than others.
Skeptic
A second, independent model (stats only, no betting line) plus warning checks. If a warning historically meant losing bets, it vetoes the bet.
Edge Lab
The testing system: a new idea is only used if it improves predictions on seasons the model never saw, passes a strict statistical test, and holds up across most seasons.
Book disagreement
How far apart sportsbooks' lines are. Big disagreement often means news, a stale line or uncertainty.
